# Private beta plan and the Discovery & Community direction

## Summary
RebirthAI moves from hackathon demo to a planned private beta with a design partner (a Google
engineer who asked the team to make it real) and people he knows. A new plan sets the bar for
inviting anyone, five new Issues cover the gaps, and the owner's next feature space, Discovery
and Community, is recorded as the phase after the beta with its principles fixed up front.

## Context
Owner direction on 2026-10-27: (1) the hackathon project has a real prospective user base;
(2) after writing a testimony, people should be able to browse others, get a "for you" feed of
related testimonies, and find local Bible studies and churches they may fit. The SDD had
discovery as an open question (OQ-04) and out of MVP scope (D-05).

## What changed
- `docs/BETA_PLAN.md` (new): goal, must-have bar for inviting anyone, owner decisions in order,
  and the Discovery & Community phase with its agreed principles.
- New Issues (Backlog, Human Review = Pending): #25 privacy policy and terms, #26 delete my account
  and all my data, #27 reports reach the operator with a takedown path, #28 feedback link,
  #29 design Discovery & Community (SDD v0.2 section). Appended to `docs/issues.json` as I-21–I-25.
- `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`: decisions D-06 (listing source), D-07 (feed consent model),
  D-08 (team and ownership); Issue table rows for #25–#29.
- `docs/ROADMAP.md`: "Where we are" now names the beta; phase 8 Discovery & Community added;
  discovery removed from "Later".
- `CLAUDE.md`: "hackathon, favor demos" replaced by "heading to a private beta; never cut corners
  on privacy, data or the coach's truthfulness".

## Decisions and alternatives
- **Beta before Discovery** (owner agreed): a feed needs content and the matching needs real
  user insight; building Discovery first would launch an empty feed on sensitive data.
- **Discovery principles fixed now**: opt-in only, Unlisted never shown, existing testimonies never
  added automatically; suggest, never judge fit; privacy and security review before build.
- **Discovery is one design Issue, not build Issues**: building without a design would be guessing.
- **Phase field**: the Project has no "Discovery" Phase option. Adding one through the API replaces
  all options and risks clearing existing items' phases, so #29's Phase is left blank for the owner.

## Assumptions and open questions
- Beta size of 10–20 people is an estimate, to be set with the design partner.
- D-06, D-07 and D-08 are open; D-03 and D-04 remain the first technical decisions.
